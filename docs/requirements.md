# Requirements

Written down after launch, from what the site does today. Update it before starting new work.

## Goal

Let customers of Teambull Fuel House, a supplement shop in Puducherry, browse genuine products and order on WhatsApp, and give the shop one place to manage stock and make bills.

## Users

- **Customers:** browse, use the free fitness tools, add to cart, order on WhatsApp, optionally keep an account.
- **Shop staff (admin):** manage products, prices and stock, see orders, make and cancel bills at the counter.

## Features

- Product catalogue with categories, flavours, sizes, MRP and savings, and live stock
- Cart and checkout that opens WhatsApp with the order typed in
- Customer accounts; orders saved for logged-in customers
- Admin: products, stock, order status, billing counter with print and PDF, cancelled bills
- Free tools: BMI calculator, protein calculator, supplement finder
- Play & Win games with badges and a certificate
- About, contact and policy pages; SEO and share images
- Seasonal Christmas decorations behind one switch

## Out of scope (today)

- Online payment on the site (orders are confirmed on WhatsApp)
- Forgot-password emails
- Delivery tracking
